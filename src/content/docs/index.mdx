## What is Putrajaya of Roblox?

<p class="pw-intro">Putrajaya of Roblox is a Ro-State roleplay community built around public service, city life, and the stories citizens create together. This wiki is the shared reference: how to join, which departments exist, what the rules are, and how the city's systems fit together. Every page is maintained by the community and reviewed before it goes live.</p>

## Frequently asked

<div class="pw-faq">

  <div>
    <h3>How do I join Putrajaya of Roblox?</h3>
    <p>Start with the Getting Started guide, then join the community spaces and introduce yourself. No application is needed to be a citizen.</p>
  </div>

  <div>
    <h3>Do I need to apply for a role?</h3>
    <p>Some departments recruit through the community Discord. The Roles and Departments article explains what each role involves before you commit.</p>
  </div>

  <div>
    <h3>How do I contribute to the wiki?</h3>
    <p>Every article is Markdown in the GitHub repository. Use the "Edit page" link at the bottom of any article to propose a change.</p>
  </div>

  <div>
    <h3>Something is out of date. How do I report it?</h3>
    <p>Open an issue on the repository, or edit the page directly. Changes are reviewed before they go live.</p>
  </div>

</div>

<div class="pw-cta">
  <div>
    <h3>Want to help keep this accurate?</h3>
