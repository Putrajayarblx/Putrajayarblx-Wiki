## What is Putrajaya of Roblox?

<p class="pw-intro">Putrajaya.rblx (also known as Putrajaya of Roblox) is a Roblox Ro-State roleplay community built around realism and authenthic roleplaying experiences, featuring the iconic administrative city of Putrajaya as its main focal point. Founded in 2024 as a small group of few, we have since grown into a large community with partnerships alongside other renowned Roblox Malaysian RP communities to deliver an experience never before seen.</p>
