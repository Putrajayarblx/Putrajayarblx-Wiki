The city runs on departments. This section explains what each role actually does, who you report to, and how a normal shift looks.

Joining a department is a cycle, not a race. Read the role you want, wait for recruitment to open, and show up prepared.

## Before you apply

- Know which department fits your play style.
- Read that department's article in this section.
- Be active in the community spaces — recruitment often favours familiar faces.

## Your first shift

Follow your supervisor's instructions, stay in character, and ask questions in the department channel rather than in global chat.

:::tip
You can hold a citizen role while you wait. Nothing about joining a department requires you to be idle first.
:::
