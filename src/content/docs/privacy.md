Putrajaya.rblx Wiki is a static, community-maintained reference. It has no accounts, comments, or reader uploads.

## Privacy

The wiki stores a few preferences in your browser's local storage:

- theme, text size, and content width;
- your "Was this page helpful?" vote for each article.

These stay on your device and are not sent to the server. Clear your browser storage to remove them.

## Analytics

The wiki uses Vercel Web Analytics and Speed Insights to measure aggregate traffic and performance. They are cookieless and do not build a profile of you.

When you vote on an article, the wiki sends a custom `helpful` event containing only the vote and the article path. It does not include an account, email address, or other identity.

Vercel processes request metadata such as IP address and user agent to serve and protect the site. See [Vercel's privacy policy](https://vercel.com/legal/privacy-policy) for details.

## Cookies

The wiki does not set advertising or analytics cookies. Vercel may set infrastructure cookies needed to serve the site securely.
